ABOUT ME

Learning to perceive,
understand, and act.

I am a master’s student at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Haoqian Wang. I received my bachelor’s degree from the School of Electronics and Information at Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2025.

My current research focuses on vision-language-action (VLA) models and world models (WM). I am interested in how these models represent the world and support intelligent interaction.

I am currently a research intern at XPeng, exploring methods for evaluating long-horizon consistency in multi-view world models.
